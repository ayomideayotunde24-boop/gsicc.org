Lives Changed by Christ

Hear from members whose lives have been transformed by the power of God through the ministry of Good Shepherd International Christian Churches.

"
I came to Good Shepherd broken, homeless, and addicted to drugs. I had lost everything. The unconditional love I experienced was overwhelming — people didn't judge my past. They saw me as God saw me: valuable and worth saving. Three years later, I've been clean, restored relationships with my children, and now have steady employment. Today I serve in the homeless ministry, offering hope to others where I once was.
Sarah O.
Freedom from Addiction
"
Our marriage was on the brink of divorce. After 12 years together we had grown apart. A friend invited us to GSICC's marriage conference, and we reluctantly agreed as a "last resort." That weekend changed everything. God began healing what we thought was beyond repair. Five years later, our marriage is stronger than ever — and we now serve as marriage mentors, helping other couples find what we found here.
The Martinez Family
Healed Marriage
"
When my husband of 45 years suddenly passed away, I didn't know how I would survive. GSICC's care ministry immediately surrounded me with support. The GriefShare group became my lifeline. Three years later, I facilitate a widow support group, mentor younger women, and went on a mission trip to Guatemala — something I'd never have imagined at 67. God's plan for my life didn't end with my loss.
Grace A.
Purpose After Loss
"
I was a committed atheist with a Ph.D. in physics who believed faith and science were incompatible. I attended an apologetics class expecting to expose the flaws in Christianity. Instead I encountered well-reasoned arguments and authentic faith in action that my worldview couldn't explain. I surrendered my life to Christ on Easter Sunday. I now lead the church's apologetics ministry, helping others find rational reasons for faith.
David K.
From Atheist to Believer
"
As a high school freshman I struggled with anxiety, depression, and questions about my identity. Suicide seemed like the only escape. A friend brought me to youth group. The leaders genuinely cared — they created a safe space to ask hard questions. I learned I was loved, valued, and created with purpose. I've now served on two mission trips and I'm planning to study youth ministry in college, to offer other teenagers the hope I found here.
James T.
